About Me

I'm studying Web & Digital Development and have spent several years working with networking, Linux, system testing, and technical troubleshooting. Along the way, I've become more interested in programming and building projects that people can actually use.

I like clean designs that are easy to understand. I would rather keep a website organized and functional than add extra effects that distract from the content.
